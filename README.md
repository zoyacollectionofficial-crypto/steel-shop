# SteelDesk — Iron & Steel Shop Management

Premium glassmorphism web app for iron & steel shops: products (Sariya, girders,
pipes, sheets…), inventory with Ton/KG/Piece conversion, sales & purchase
invoices (A4 / 80mm / 58mm print), customer & supplier ledgers, payments,
expenses, profit & reports — in PKR, with English/Urdu interface.

**Login:** admin / admin123 (change it in Shop Settings → Users after first login).
Data is saved in the browser on the device (localStorage) — use Settings →
Backup & Restore to download/restore a full backup.
